# AI Compliance Defaults

Applies when a frontend feature uses an LLM, recommender, generative output, or automated decision.

- Every model-produced output carries a visible AI disclosure label; a chatbot never claims to be human.
- Never ship EU AI Act Art. 5 uses: social scoring, workplace or school emotion recognition, public real-time biometric ID.
- Decisions with significant effect offer human review, a plain explanation, and appeal.
- Photorealistic generated media of a real person carries a permanent watermark.

Full rule, examples, and rationale: [`standards/ai-compliance-defaults.md`](../standards/ai-compliance-defaults.md). Read it before building any AI-facing feature.
