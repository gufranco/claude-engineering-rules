# Privacy Defaults

GDPR-grade by default everywhere, for any task that touches personal data.

- Consent is opt-in, granular, and withdrawable; never bundled or pre-ticked.
- DSAR in 30 days; erasure means gone or irreversibly anonymized, never a soft-delete flag.
- Breach notice within 72 hours; retention 24 months unless under legal hold.
- Never put personal data in logs, URLs, error messages, or non-production data; pseudonymous data is personal.

Full rule, examples, and rationale: [`standards/privacy-defaults.md`](../standards/privacy-defaults.md). Read it before collecting, storing, or displaying personal data.
