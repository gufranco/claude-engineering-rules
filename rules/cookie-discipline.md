# Cookie Discipline

Applies to any cookie, localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, or tracker.

- Non-essential cookies and trackers load only after opt-in; analytics is not essential.
- "Reject all" takes as many clicks as "Accept all"; no pre-ticked boxes, no cookie walls.
- The banner is an accessible modal dialog; withdrawal is one click from any page.
- Never hardcode a tracker ID or fire a marketing pixel without a consent guard.

Full rule, examples, and rationale: [`standards/cookie-discipline.md`](../standards/cookie-discipline.md). Read it before setting client-side storage or building a consent banner.
