# Anti-Spam and Marketing Defaults

Applies to any email, SMS, push, or in-app message, marketing or transactional.

- Marketing requires opt-in; never pre-check the box.
- One-click unsubscribe with no login, a `List-Unsubscribe` header per RFC 8058, and a permanent suppression list.
- Every commercial email shows the sender's legal name and postal address; subject lines never deceive.
- Promotion beyond a small footer turns a transactional email into marketing.

Full rule, examples, and rationale: [`standards/anti-spam-defaults.md`](../standards/anti-spam-defaults.md). Read it before building any outbound messaging.
