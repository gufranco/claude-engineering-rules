# Children's Privacy Defaults

Applies when a product targets, attracts, or may be used by anyone under 18.

- Age 18 for profiling and behavioral ads, 16 for accounts, verifiable parental consent under 13.
- Under-18 defaults are maximum privacy: geolocation, sharing, search, and stranger messaging off.
- No streaks, "active now" indicators, or nudges to share more or stay longer.
- Prefer behavioral age estimation over ID upload.

Full rule, examples, and rationale: [`standards/children-privacy-defaults.md`](../standards/children-privacy-defaults.md). Read it before building sign-up, age gates, or social features.
