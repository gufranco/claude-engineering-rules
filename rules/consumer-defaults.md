# Consumer and E-commerce Defaults

Applies to any flow that sells, subscribes, or forms a contract with a user.

- Show the total price with taxes and fees on first display, never first at checkout.
- 14-day no-reason withdrawal and refund within 14 days; cancelling takes no more clicks than subscribing.
- No pre-selected add-ons, fake scarcity, fake countdowns, or undisclosed incentivized reviews.
- Seller legal name, contact, and complaint channel visible from every page.

Full rule, examples, and rationale: [`standards/consumer-defaults.md`](../standards/consumer-defaults.md). Read it before building checkout, pricing, or subscription flows.
